Coordinate your agent fleet for software delivery.

Refine is not another agent harness — it is the management layer on top of your agents. It turns product feedback into Goals, dispatches agents through planning and implementation, and keeps files, terminals, nodes, processes, evidence, Git, and review close to your local workflow.

Why Refine

Ways to coordinate software delivery.

Direct agents means using a coding agent without an added orchestration platform; capabilities vary by agent. Blitzy specializes in autonomous software development. n8n connects tools through general-purpose workflows and AI automation. Refine coordinates software delivery around your existing repositories and installed agents.

What Refine does

Your team's agentic software delivery system.

01

Empower your team

Extend product feedback and app edits to your whole team. Keep planning, features, Goals, chat, and human verification in one workflow. Enter, success, error, and exit events connect your Skills; shared controls let you recover with context when a step fails. Failed work waits for an explicit decision. Manage Governance, Quality, and shared guidance through Skills.

02

Agent fleet

Orchestrate your agent fleet across decentralized repositories, branches, processes, and your fleet of nodes. Connect any MCP client to the always-on local endpoint, and cheap feedback loops keep agent work moving while git keeps everyone in sync.

03

Governance

Keep agent work aligned with your product intent, local rules, and requirements. Review stays grounded before changes merge.

04

Hub

Keep reports, historical records, and static sites with your app's state. Manage them from Hubs in the left rail, the CLI, API, or MCP, and publish reports as websites hosted by Refine.
